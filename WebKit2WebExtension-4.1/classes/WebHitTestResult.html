WebKit2WebExtension.WebHitTestResultClass

Result of a Hit Test (Web Process Extensions).

WebKit2WebExtension.WebHitTestResult extends WebKit2WebExtension.HitTestResult to provide information about the WebKit2WebExtension.DOMNode in the coordinates of the Hit Test.

Added in version 2.8.

get_js_node(world)¶
Parameters:

world (WebKit2WebExtension.ScriptWorld or None) – a WebKit2WebExtension.ScriptWorld, or None to use the default

Returns:

a JavaScriptCore.Value for the DOM node, or None

Return type:

JavaScriptCore.Value or None

Get the JavaScriptCore.Value for the DOM node in world at the coordinates of the Hit Test.

Added in version 2.40.
