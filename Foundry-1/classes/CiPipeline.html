Foundry.CiPipelineClass

Abstract representation of a continuous integration pipeline.

CI providers subclass FoundryCiPipeline to expose provider-specific pipelines and their jobs. The properties are read-only. Subclasses whose values can change must emit notify for the affected property and emit [signal`Gio`.ListModel::items-changed] from the model returned by [method`Foundry`.CiPipeline.list_jobs].

Added in version 1.2.
