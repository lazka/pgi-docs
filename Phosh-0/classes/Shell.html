Structure:

Phosh.ShellClass

The shell singleton

Phosh.Shell is responsible for instantiating the GUI parts of the shell#PhoshTopPanel, #PhoshHome,… and the managers that interface with DBus #PhoshMonitorManager, #PhoshFeedbackManager, … and coordinates between them.
