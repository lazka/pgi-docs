Phosh.LockscreenManagerClass

The singleton that manages screen locking

The Phosh.LockscreenManager is responsible for putting the Phosh.Lockscreen on the primary output and a #PhoshLockshield on other outputs when the session becomes idle or when invoked explicitly via Phosh.LockscreenManager.set_locked().
