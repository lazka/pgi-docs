get_publisher()¶
Returns:

A Infinity.XmlConnection, or None.

Infinity.XmlConnection or None

Returns a Infinity.XmlConnection to the group’s publisher, or None if the publisher is no longer a group member.

remove_member(connection)¶
Parameters:

connection (Infinity.XmlConnection) – A connection to a member of self.

Removes connection as self's member. On the remote site, the corresponding Infinity.CommunicationGroup needs to be freed (which may be a Infinity.CommunicationJoinedGroup or a Infinity.CommunicationHostedGroup).
