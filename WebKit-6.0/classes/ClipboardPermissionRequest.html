WebKit.ClipboardPermissionRequestClass

A permission request for reading clipboard contents.

WebKit.ClipboardPermissionRequest represents a request for permission to decide whether WebKit can access the clipboard to read its contents through the Async Clipboard API.

When a WebKit.ClipboardPermissionRequest is not handled by the user, it is denied by default.

Added in version 2.42.
